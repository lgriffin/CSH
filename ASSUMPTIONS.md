# Assumptions register

Every provisional assumption made during the build (Implementer's brief, rule 2). Each one names what it assumes,
the code it affects and how to reverse it. Where the documents were silent or ambiguous, the most conservative
option was taken: the one that yields unknown, rejects, or constrains least. Output that depends on an assumption is
provisional until the owner confirms it. The questions behind them are in [QUESTIONS.md](QUESTIONS.md).

| A-01 | The report and gate records carry extra fields beyond those the documents list. In the report these are `query` on a finding, `unliftable`, `errors`, `diagnostics`, `executions`, `items`, `ledger` and `composition`. In the gate decision they are `recommends`, `because` and `candidates`. Every field the documents name keeps its meaning. | `packages/check/src/types.ts`, `packages/gate/src/gate.ts` | Remove the fields marked "Extension" | Q-15 |
