# Assumptions register

Every provisional assumption made during the build (Implementer's brief, rule 2). Each one names what it assumes,
the code it affects and how to reverse it. Where the documents were silent or ambiguous, the most conservative
option was taken: the one that yields unknown, rejects, or constrains least. The questions behind them are in
[QUESTIONS.md](QUESTIONS.md).

Every assumption that answers a question was confirmed by the owner on 3 October 2026, and is marked "confirmed" in the last
column. An assumption with no question is an implementation detail inside what the documents already decide. It stays
provisional, and changing it needs no owner decision.

A-35 to A-37 are the three assumptions the anchor design takes so that its build is not blocked
([09](docs/spec/09-anchor-harness-a3.md), section 14.2). The owner can reverse each; the last column of that table, copied
into "How to reverse" here, says what a reversal costs.
